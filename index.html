Career Hierarchy: Space Scientist vs. Astronaut

Space Scientist / Engineer: B.Sc. or B.Tech → M.Sc., M.Tech, or Ph.D. / research exams → Space agency design, payload, or mission teams.

Astronaut / Gaganyatri: STEM, engineering, or pilot degree → Medical, psychological, and technical selection → Centrifuge, simulator, and mission training.
